# Aura – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 November 2026*

Aura is an Auto DJ music player for Windows, made by LuvDup in the United Kingdom.
This policy explains what information Aura uses and where it goes. The short version:
**Aura has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. Your music and your library stay on your computer.**

## What Aura stores on your device

To work, Aura keeps the following on your own computer only:

- **Your music library** – the file names and locations of the tracks you add, and the tags inside them (title, artist, album, cover art).
- **Track analysis** – tempo, beat positions, key, loudness, energy and similar details Aura works out by listening to your tracks.
- **Your settings and history** – skin, volume, keyboard shortcuts, mix style, set plans, what has played recently, and anything you teach Aura in learning mode.
- **Recordings** – if you record a mix, the audio file is saved where you choose.
- **Now Playing files** – if you use the streaming/OBS feature, the current track's title, artist and cover are written to `Documents\Aura\Now Playing` and shown on a page that only your own computer can open (`127.0.0.1`).

None of this is sent to LuvDup or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing your library in Aura or uninstalling it.

## When Aura connects to the internet

Aura plays and analyses your music entirely on your device, works fully offline and never contacts LuvDup or anyone else.

Aura is installed and kept up to date by the **Microsoft Store**. Those downloads and updates are handled by Microsoft under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); Aura itself does not check for updates or send anything online.

## Permissions

Aura only accesses the music folders and files you choose to add.

Aura does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

## Information LuvDup collects

None. LuvDup does not receive, collect, sell or share any personal information from Aura.
If you contact LuvDup (for example to report a problem), the details you send are used only to reply to you.

## Children

Aura does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you have rights over your personal information. Because Aura keeps everything on your own computer and LuvDup holds nothing about you, you stay in control of it: you can view, change or delete it directly in Aura or by uninstalling the app.

## Changes to this policy

If Aura ever starts using information differently, this policy will be updated first and the date at the top changed.
